## Challenge

The firm needed a professional web presence that could do more than describe services: it needed to qualify prospects, set expectations before money changed hands, and move a visitor from curiosity to a signed, paid engagement without manual back-and-forth.

## Approach

MBMApps designed and built a custom site around three service lines — credit consulting, document and filing support, and business education — with a self-guided assessment prospects can complete before booking, and a booking flow that carries them from inquiry through a written proposal, deposit collection, and work authorization.

## Result

The firm now runs on a single branded site that educates prospects, qualifies fit through the assessment tool, books consultations, and collects deposits under a transparent, documented engagement process — replacing scattered outreach with one guided commercial path.
