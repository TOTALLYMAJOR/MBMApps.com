## Challenge

The business depended on a single-host deployment model that lacked confidence under incident conditions.

## Approach

We introduced a Docker-first service architecture, consistent environment contracts, and smoke checks per deployment target.

## Result

The team gained deployment flexibility and reduced operational anxiety with a tested portability strategy.
