## Challenge

The team relied on spreadsheets, email threads, and manual approvals. Pricing drift and inconsistent quote formats made it hard to close fast.

## Approach

MBMApps shipped a typed Next.js workflow with a Railway API and Firebase-backed user access. We introduced a reusable pricing engine, approval statuses, and analytics instrumentation.

## Result

- Quote cycle time dropped dramatically.
- Sales leadership gained a live conversion funnel.
- Team confidence improved due to a stable release process and preview environments.
